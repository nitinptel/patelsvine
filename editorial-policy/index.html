Editorial Policy

Read PatelsVine's editorial standards for sourcing, corrections, neutrality, and content quality.

Source standards

We prefer primary sources such as official releases, Election Commission data, ministry notes, public reports, and direct event announcements. When using media reports, we use them for context and avoid copying protected expression.

Original writing

Every article is written in original language. We summarise facts, explain implications, and link sources so readers can check the basis of the article.

Corrections

If an error is identified, we update the article, correct the relevant sentence, and adjust the updated date where appropriate. Send correction requests through the contact page.

Political coverage

Political articles are written in a neutral explanatory tone. We distinguish official results and verified facts from interpretation, and we avoid personal attacks or unverified allegations.
